Six people live here. None of them speak your language.

The villagers are played by a language model, so the game needs an API key before it can start. It is kept in this browser and sent only to the provider you pick below.

The key lives in this browser's local storage and goes only to the provider above. Anyone with access to your browser profile can read it, so use a key you are happy to rotate. If you opened this page as a file, serve it over http://localhost instead — browsers block API calls from file:// pages.

Little Village

A small place where nobody speaks your language. You have to talk your way through it.

  • WASD or arrow keys to walk.
  • E or Space to talk to whoever you are standing next to.
  • In a conversation, type in their language. The Phrases row gives you something to start from, and Offer an item holds something out from your pockets.
  • They remember what you tell them — and they repeat it to each other.
  • Your notebook only fills in with things a villager has actually told you. If it is empty, go and ask.

Where to start: somebody in this village wants something, and somebody else has it. Nobody will volunteer that — knock on doors and ask. Every village is a new errand; the one you are in is named at the bottom of your notebook.

Change the language, the difficulty or the model at any time with the ⚙ button at the top of the screen.

🎉 The errand is done

Wander back out and keep chatting: the villagers still remember everything you told them, and they are still gossiping about you.
